FPGACenter sources hard-to-find, obsolete, and end-of-life electronic components, with
deep FPGA, CPLD, IC, semiconductor, passive, and connector coverage. The site supports
RFQs, no minimum order quantity, global shipping, and inspection-oriented sourcing for

Long description:

FPGACenter is an electronic component sourcing platform focused on hard-to-find,
obsolete, end-of-life, and legacy production parts. The catalog covers 720K+ part
numbers across major semiconductor and component manufacturers, including strong FPGA
and CPLD coverage. For active products, confirmed stocked inventory may support fast
dispatch. For obsolete, EOL, and NRND parts, FPGACenter verifies availability,
provenance, lead time, and pricing through qualified specialty sourcing channels before
confirming supply. RFQs are supported with no minimum order quantity and global express
shipping options.

Category keywords:

Quality statement:

FPGACenter positions sourcing around original/genuine components, supplier screening,
traceability documentation, and inspection-oriented procurement. Avoid using any
certification logo or membership logo unless the membership is approved and the logo use
is explicitly permitted by that organization.
